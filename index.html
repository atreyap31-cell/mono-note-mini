Welcome — first boot

This looks like your very first time opening Mono Note Mini. Here's the 30-second tour — everything is black & white, paper-ruled, and yours alone.

1 Tap + New note or hit the mic to record — it saves instantly and appears in the list.
2 Click any note to edit the Courier transcript — it autosaves and lines up on the ruled paper.
3 Tap a bubbly tag in the top bar to filter, or tap a chip in the reader to file a note.
4 Use the search box to find anything by name, text, or tag.
5 Settings → Speech speed to change how fast it reads aloud, and Typewriter sounds to make typing feel nice.

Tip: your notes sync with wav audio (<8 MB) to GitHub when you set owner/repo/token in Settings → GitHub sync. No server needed for speaking or live transcription.
