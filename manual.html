Mono Note Mini

A notebook you talk to. Press a button, say the thing, and it is filed — on a card in your pocket, not on anyone's server.

1 · What it is

A voice recorder with one button. Press it, say the thing, press it again. The note is kept on a memory card inside the device, and copied to your own computer when it is in range of your Wi-Fi.

It does not have menus, settings, or an account. It cannot show you your notes — that is what the website is for. It is deliberately the least interesting object in your pocket.

2 · Using it

  1. Press the top button to wake it.
  2. Press the top button again to start recording. A filled circle appears.
  3. Say what you need to say.
  4. Press it once more to stop. The circle becomes a square and the screen says SAVED.

That is everything. The bottom button wakes it and does nothing else.

Two minutes is the limit A single note can be two minutes long. It stops on its own and tells you, rather than quietly going deaf part way through.

3 · What the screen tells you

no notes yetNothing recorded.
3 of 7 syncedSeven notes on the card; three have reached your computer. The other four go up next time it has Wi-Fi.
all syncedEverything is safely copied.
a filled circleRecording now.
a square, then SAVEDIt stopped and wrote the file.

The bar along the top is the battery, in quarters. The screen keeps its picture even when the device is asleep or flat — e-paper costs nothing to hold an image — so what you see is what it last said, not a sign that it is awake.

4 · Battery and charging

Charge it with any USB-C cable. Plugging it in charges the battery and powers the device at the same time, and it stays awake while plugged in instead of sleeping on its usual timer.

Left alone it goes to sleep after half a minute, which is where nearly all of its battery life comes from.

5 · Setting it up, once

The device has nowhere to type a Wi-Fi password, so this happens on the website over Bluetooth. It is the only setup there is, and you do it once.

  1. Open the website on a computer, in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Set up device over Bluetooth and pick Mono Note Mini.
  3. Press Scan — the device lists the networks it can actually see — and pick yours.
  4. Type the password and save.

Or skip the password entirely: press Use my router's button, then press the WPS button on your router within two minutes. The router hands the device the network itself.

Which browsers Bluetooth from a web page works in Chrome and Edge on a computer, and Chrome on Android. It does not work on an iPhone or iPad — Safari does not support it, and neither does Chrome on iOS, because Apple requires every iOS browser to use Safari's engine.

6 · The website

There is one, and it is where everything that is not recording happens: reading your notes, searching them, tagging them, having them written out as text, and hearing them read back.

Notes arrive there by themselves. The device uploads each one to the computer running the site as soon as it has Wi-Fi, and the site pulls them in. Nothing goes to anyone else's server, and there is no account anywhere.

7 · Your notes are files

Every note is an ordinary .wav file on an ordinary memory card, named by the date and time it was made. Take the card out and put it in a computer and there they are — no software, no export, no format anyone else controls.

This is the point. If the website stopped existing tomorrow, and this manual with it, your notes would still open in anything that plays sound.

8 · If something is wrong

The screen never changes when I press

The screen holds its last picture with no power at all, so a flat battery looks exactly like a working one. Charge it for an hour and try again.

It says NOT SAVED

The memory card could not be written to. Check it is pushed all the way in, and that it is not switched to read-only.

The browser shows no device to pick

Wake the device first — it only talks over Bluetooth while awake. Then check your browser: this needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on Android. It cannot work on an iPhone or iPad.

The counter never reaches "all synced"

The device only uploads when it can reach the computer running the website. Check that computer is switched on, that the server is running, and that the device is in range of the same Wi-Fi.

If your computer's address changes, the device will keep trying to reach the old one. Give it a fixed address on your router, or set the new one from the website.

It cannot find my Wi-Fi

The device uses 2.4 GHz networks. If yours is set to 5 GHz only, it will not appear in the scan.

9 · Looking after it

  • Keep it dry. It is not sealed.
  • Do not leave it in a hot car. The battery dislikes heat more than anything else.
  • Charge it every few weeks if you are not using it. A battery left completely flat for months may not come back.
  • Do not press on the screen. The glass is thin, and it is the one part that cannot be replaced.
  • Back up occasionally. Copy the card's contents to a computer now and then, or set up syncing so it happens by itself. Memory cards do fail.
